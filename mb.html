MB · Mama's Brain

Conscious intelligence for expert workflows.

Mama's Brain builds tools where experts and AI systems work on the same objects, and the human keeps control of every one of them. Parent company to slAIdes, EasyPlotter, AgentPortfolio and GeniusLamp. Wings, not autopilot.

Mama's Brain is the parent company. Its products share one principle: the expert keeps the object, the AI does the labour, and nothing is ever flattened into something you cannot edit or interrogate.

Status · Founding from the KTH Innovation ecosystem, Stockholm. Bootstrapped. First product live and charging: slAIdes.

The family

One principle, four products.

Live · slaides.app

slAIdes

An AI-native presentation workspace. LaTeX stays a live, re-editable slide object instead of a flattened picture; transitions apply themselves; image and video editing happen on the canvas; and the AI editor restyles one slide or the whole deck, always as a change you accept or discard.

EasyPlotter

Scientific data visualisation inside VS Code. Load CSV, HDF5, NumPy or Excel, build multi-series plots with processing pipelines, and export publication-ready figures without leaving the editor.

AgentPortfolio

Portfolio review for public markets: live market data, news analysis and agent coordination turned into an auditable basis a professional can defend. Human-in-the-loop review, not automatic trading.

GeniusLamp

Opportunity review for private markets: company material, evidence, missing proof and reviewer feedback become a structured decision packet, with a historical-validation harness behind the scoring.

Principle

Nothing gets flattened

An equation stays an equation, a chart keeps its data, a score stays interrogable. If you cannot edit it or argue with it, we have not finished building it.

Principle

Wings, not autopilot

AI that augments expert judgment instead of replacing it: measurable, auditable, and honest about what it does not know.
