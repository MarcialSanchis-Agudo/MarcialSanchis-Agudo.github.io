What we commit to

Three promises, one craft.

Within five years, using AI in expert work should no longer mean giving up authorship. Speed and control stop being a trade-off.

01

Relearning to learn

What the AI does stays open, visible and editable, so working with it makes you better at your craft instead of making you forget it.

02

Coherent workflows

One thread from source to result: objects, proposals and the memory of decisions carry across steps and tools.

03

Work that lasts

Work you can come back to months later, review, reuse and defend, at a pace an expert can sustain.

Our design rule

Nothing gets flattened.

An equation stays an equation, a chart keeps its data, a claim keeps its source. No system certifies its own work: the machine proposes, the expert decides.

Our first product · live

Technical talks where LaTeX stays LaTeX.

  • Equations stay LaTeX: click one, edit the source, see it update.

How we build

Software has a new user. We built for both.

People edit with their hands. AI agents edit through MCP, an open protocol: typed operations the server checks before they land, each one a numbered revision. The AI doesn't get a brush; it gets a toolbox, and you keep the last word.

The structure is ours; the model on the other side can change.
